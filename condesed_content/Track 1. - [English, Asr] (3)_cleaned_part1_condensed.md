# Course Introduction and Cybersecurity Overview

## Course Site - modules, announcements, assessments, trimester overview, events, suggestion box.

## Recordings - Collaborate recordings available for review; chat recorded; do not publish materials.

## Attendance - not directly assessed; keep up with recordings; attend reserved in-person workshops if enrolled.

## Weekly Quizzes - 8 assessed workshop quizzes; 40% total; 5% each; due following Wednesday 5:00 pm.

## Self-Review Quizzes - practice only, not assessed.

## Final Exam - 60%, online proctored, no chat/ChatGPT, one A4 double-sided note sheet allowed.

## Exam Hurdle - must score over 40% on final to pass course.

## Weekly Pattern - pre-lecture recordings, lecture, workshop, activity, quiz.

## Module Contents - slides, recordings, activities, workshop material, quiz.

## Learning Activities - supplementary, useful for non-IT background, not final-exam assessed.

## Linux/Unix/VMs - workshops use Linux; local VM optional; cloud service provided.

## Local VM Limits - needs disk/RAM; two VMs slow older computers; M1+ Macs may have setup issues.

## Cloud Accounts - avoid local VM resource/compatibility problems.

## SEED Labs - Professor Wenliang Du cybersecurity labs used for selected workshops/resources.

## Week One Workshop - no formal workshop; explore intro resources.

## Ethical Use - course tools only for learning; no illegal sniffing/cracking.

## Course Coverage - attackers, methods, cyberspace protection.

## Three Dimensions - people, technology, policy.

## Technology - main focus; threats, protections, tools, standards.

## Policy - standards and constraints for proper technology use.

## People - user behavior and awareness determine protection effectiveness.

## Cybersecurity Relationship - technology, policy, and people all required.

## Study Discipline - keep pace to understand quiz answers, workshops, and theory.

# Lec1Rec1: CIA Model and Core Cybersecurity Goals

## CIA Model - 1974 core goals: confidentiality, integrity, availability.

## Expanded Goals - CIA plus authenticity and non-repudiation.

## Confidentiality - only authorized people access data; protected by encryption, authentication, access control.

## Data Classification - public, internal, confidential.

## Integrity - data/software not changed, corrupted, or manipulated without authorization.

## Integrity Controls - change monitoring, permissions, auditing/accounting.

## Availability - systems, services, data, users, and apps accessible when needed.

## Availability Threats - disasters/equipment failure or attacks making services unusable.

## Authenticity - assurance of true sender/creator/origin.

## Non-Repudiation - sender/issuer cannot deny an action later; often via digital signatures.

## Software Clean-Code Scenario - integrity.

## Always-Accessible Service Scenario - availability.

## Financial Email Scenario - authenticity.

## Old Degree Certificate Scenario - non-repudiation.

## Exam Papers Scenario - confidentiality.
